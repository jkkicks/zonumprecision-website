How it works

From RFQ to your bench.

A simple, transparent process. You'll always know where your parts are and who's working on them.

  1. Send your files

    A STEP model plus a drawing for anything toleranced. Include quantity, material, finish, and when you need it.

  2. Review & quote

    I review the design for manufacturability and send a quote with a lead time, plus any suggestions that could save you money.

  3. Machine & inspect

    Programmed, fixtured, and cut. Critical features are measured against your print before anything ships.

  4. Ship & iterate

    Parts arrive clean, deburred, and packed. Setups and programs are saved, so rev B goes faster.

FAQ

Good questions,
straight answers.

Don't see yours? Email me and I'll answer it.

What should I include with an RFQ?

A STEP file, a PDF drawing with tolerances and callouts, quantity, material, finish, and your target date. If you only have a model, that's fine; I'll ask about the features that matter.

Do you take one-off prototypes?

Yes. Prototypes and development parts are a big part of what I do. I'm happy to make one part, then make the next revision when your design changes.

Should I order through Subtract or directly?

Either works. If your company already sources through the Subtract Manufacturing network, ordering there may fit your purchasing process. Otherwise, email me directly and we'll work it out.

What exactly is a vibe code cleanup?

You (or an AI tool) built something quickly, and it mostly works. I go through it, find the security problems, broken patterns, and missing tests, fix what's risky, and leave you with clear docs and a codebase a team can maintain.

How do software projects get priced?

Usually a fixed-scope audit first, so you know exactly what you're dealing with. From there, cleanup and build work is quoted per project, with clear deliverables and no open-ended hourly surprises.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send it over with your RFQ and I'll review it before you share files.
